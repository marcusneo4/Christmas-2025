# Surface: Lucky draw app (all screens)

Mode: Experience. It's performed on a TV for a room of family, so the show is the product.
Scope: setup, draw (Last Light Standing), game-player picker (same tree), results. One-page static app.
Job: the host loads names and prizes, then presses Space per prize; the room watches its own names survive or go dark.
Memorable moment: silence after the spotlight stops, then the winner's name flies up and the star ignites while the whole tree relights.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every family member is a bulb on one giant tree; lights go out in waves until one name lights the star. Refuses the instant reveal (spin, then a name) that makes raffles flat: everyone stays invested because their own name might still be glowing.

OWN-WORLD: A dark living room at night: deep navy-black ground, a pine-green layered tree, C9 Christmas bulbs (red, amber, green, blue, pink, yellow) that really glow, warm cream type, gold reserved for the star, winners and the prize ladder. Big Shoulders condensed for names and prizes; Archivo for running text. Components: bulb-on-wire labels on sagging garlands, finalist cards, a stage spotlight cone, the star with rotating rays, a winner plate on a dark scrim.

STORY: The prize is announced over a dark tree. Space lights it top to bottom. The waves go 64 → 32 → 16 → 8 → 4 while a heartbeat speeds up and a drone rises. The final four lift onto cards, flicker, and two go dark. The final two step forward and the spotlight ping-pongs, slowing (sometimes nudging over at the last moment). Then silence, and the winner lights the star.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column: prize rank and prize name huge, a live count of "lights on", the host cue, and recent winners. Right two-thirds: the tree with the star at top centre, names on garlands, the trunk at the base. Key hints at bottom right.

FORM: User-chosen suspense concept "Last Light Standing" (picked over Snow Globe Shake and Pass the Parcel in an overdrive proposal round). It replaces the earlier Live Draw world, which the user rejected as lacking suspense. seed: none (pinned)

SIGNATURE: Waves of lights flicking out with falling clicks and a power-down thump; the decelerating spotlight with roulette ticks and an optional nudge; the star ignition with a celesta run, sleigh bells and a warm chord.

Sound rules: no looping music; everything goes through a soft reverb and compressor; heartbeat and drone carry the tension, and silence before the reveal is deliberate.
